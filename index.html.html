Privacy

Nothing leaves this page.

No upload

Your file is read into this page's memory and edited there. Closing the tab discards it.

No account

There is nothing to sign up for, and no history of what you opened.

No metadata

Exports are re-encoded from pixels, so camera details and GPS position in the original are not carried over.

Questions

Before you start.

Which files can I open?

JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF and BMP open in every current browser. AVIF opens where the browser supports it. HEIC opens in Safari but not in most other browsers. Animated GIFs are read as a single frame.

Why did my file get bigger?

The usual causes are saving a photo as PNG, setting quality higher than the original used, or enlarging the image. For photos, try JPEG or WebP at 75–85 and keep the width at or below the original.

How large can the output be?

Up to 16,384 px on a side. Browsers also limit total canvas area, and phones often stop near 16 megapixels. If the size readout reports an error, reduce the width and height.

Why is there no quality slider for PNG?

PNG is lossless, so every pixel is kept. Its file size depends on how much detail the image has. Resize it, or switch to WebP or JPEG, to make it smaller.

What happens when I press Save?

Your browser asks you to confirm the file name before anything is written. If saving isn't available where you opened this page, the result appears as an image you can press and hold, or right-click, to save.
